## Hey, I'm Theekshana, welcome to TheekMind

I'm Theekshana Prabodha, an ICT learner and builder from Sri Lanka. I hold the NDICT (NVQ Level 5) and I'm studying for the BIT at UCSC. TheekMind is where I write down what I learn, build and fix, clearly and practically, so you can use it too.

## What I write about

- **Networking & ICT:** VLANs, subnetting, DHCP/DNS and Cisco Packet Tracer labs, step by step.
- **Development:** PHP, MySQL, VB.NET and JavaScript, from the systems I've built.
- **Linux & DevOps:** the command line, Docker and the problems I troubleshoot on my own machine.
- **Projects:** case studies: the problem, how I approached it, and the result.
- **Learning & Communication:** study methods, UCSC BIT, Toastmasters and explaining technical ideas clearly.

## What I've built

A few highlights. Each one has its own write-up in the [Projects](/search/label/Projects) section.

- **Petty Cash Management System** for the Ceylon Electricity Board, Haliela branch: a VB.NET and SQLite desktop app with role-based access, an audit trail and Sinhala/English reports.
- **POS and Inventory Management System:** PHP 8, MySQL and Docker, with three user roles, stock control and receipts.
- **Networking Packet Tracer Labs:** 15+ labs, from basic LANs to an 80-workstation enterprise network design.
