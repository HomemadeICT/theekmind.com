# Voice and Messaging

> "Clear, practical, and focused. I share what I learn, build, and explore in tech and beyond."

## Voice
- **Clear:** short sentences, plain words, one idea per paragraph.
- **Practical:** every post gives the reader something they can do or try.
- **Focused:** one topic per post. Cut anything that does not serve it.
- First person ("I learned", "I built"), written by Theekshana as himself.

## Themes
Learn, Build, Grow, in tech and beyond.

## Standard lines
- Tagline: Build the skills. Create the future.
- Intro: Hey, I'm Theekshana, welcome to TheekMind.

## Checklist before publishing
- [ ] Does it say something concrete the reader can use?
- [ ] One topic only?
- [ ] No jargon without a one-line explanation?
- [ ] Title is plain, specific, and not clickbait?
