Physical build & hardware list
  • Building 7 m wide: Staff + server room 2.8 m · Hardware 6 m · NDICT 8.8 m · Graphics 11.8 m, 30 cm walls between labs
  • Distributed access switches per lab (keeps Cat6 runs well under 100 m), one trunk uplink per lab along the wall trunking
  • TP-Link TL-ER6120 SafeStream dual-WAN VPN router
  • TP-Link TL-SG3428 JetStream 24-port L2+ managed switch · TL-SG2218 16-port L2 managed switch
  • CB Premium 6U 19" wall rack · dual 24 V 120 mm cooling fans + 24 V-2 A PSU · 3U PDU · DCP 650 VA UPS
  • Cat6 cable, RJ45 connectors, AMP Cat6 keystone jacks, single/dual faceplates, 30 mm sunk boxes, cable ties & trunking
Configuration lifecycle
  • 1 DHCP server pools → 2 Core switch (ip routing, SVIs, relay, trunks) → 3–7 access switches → 8 end devices (DHCP)
  • 9 Router0 WAN + NAT/PAT → 10 ACLs → 11 SSH → 12 port security rollout → 13 end-to-end verification
